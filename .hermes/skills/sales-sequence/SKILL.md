# Sales Sequence

Write a 3-email sequence to a local business owner. The goal is a 15-minute conversation, not a
demo.

## Rules

- Email 1: one specific observation about their business, one sentence on the offer, one
  question.
- Email 2: one number or proof point from the business context, one short ask.
- Email 3: a clean close — “should I stop?” — no guilt, no fake urgency.
- Under 110 words per email.
- No fake “I noticed your website…” unless the brief includes the observation.
- No attachments on email 1.
- No invented case study, review, revenue or deadline.
- Plain text. No HTML.

## Output

For each email:

**Subject:** …
**Body:** …
**Send:** day 0 / day 3 / day 8

Then add:

**Personalization needed** — the fields a human must fill before sending.
**Do not send if** — the conditions that should stop the sequence.
