# The 30-Day Revenue Recovery Pilot

## The promise

> You already paid for the phone to ring. We recover the jobs hiding in the calls you missed
> and the customers you already served.

This is not a lead-gen contract. It is a **revenue recovery pilot** on the book you already
own: past customers, unsold estimates, missed calls, unreviewed jobs, and inbound replies that
went cold.

## Who it is for

- Roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest, landscaping, restoration.
- 5–50 employees, $2M–$20M revenue.
- At least 50 missed calls a month, or a customer list gathering dust.
- The owner still quotes jobs and knows the customers by name.
- A real person can approve messages and handle a warm handoff.

Not a fit: no customer list, no consent, no capacity to answer a booked job, or a business
that wants volume sprayed at purchased lists.

## What happens

| Week | Work | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Connect Twilio + Resend, import the customer list, load claims, set quiet hours and consent | A live book with a policy gate |
| 1 | Reactivate past customers with consent: one specific, grounded reason to reply | Conversations, opt-outs, first bookings |
| 2 | Work unsold estimates and missed calls; route price objections to a human | Pipeline and booked estimates |
| 3 | Ask every completed job for an honest review; close the loop on every reply | Reviews and referrals |
| 4 | Read the numbers, kill what did not work, write the next 30-day plan | Owner report and renewal decision |

The OS works the queue every day. The owner presses **Run my day** and approves the handful of
messages that need a human.

## What is measured

- **Revenue recovered** — booked jobs attributed to the OS, at the customer's average ticket.
- **Booking rate** — conversations that become an appointment.
- **Speed to lead** — minutes from inbound call/text to first response.
- **Opt-out rate** — the guardrail. If it rises, the pilot fails, even if bookings rise.
- **Messages avoided** — the kernel's "wait" decisions. Fewer, better touches.
- **Owner hours returned** — the queue the owner did not have to work manually.

A **holdout** of 10–20% of eligible contacts receives no outreach for the pilot, so the lift is
measured against a real counterfactual, not a before/after story.

## The guarantee

If the pilot does not produce **at least 3 booked jobs or $10,000 in attributed revenue** in 30
days, the next month is free. Conditions:

- The customer provides a list of at least 300 past customers with lawful consent, or call
  logs with at least 50 missed calls.
- The customer answers warm handoffs within one business day.
- The customer does not change the offer, territory or capacity mid-pilot.
- Attribution is a booked appointment in the customer's calendar or scheduling system.

No guarantee on a list that does not exist, consent that was never collected, or leads that
were purchased cold.

## What we will not do

- Buy or scrape a list.
- Text someone who did not consent.
- Invent a review, a deadline, a price or a credential.
- Hide the AI. Every message identifies the business and the human behind it.
- Keep messaging after STOP.
- Pressure a vulnerable person. The kernel escalates to a human instead.
