# The 7-Minute Demo

Open **https://biodynamx-os.web.app**, click **Open the console**. Use the demo book
(Summit Ridge Roofing, 16 contacts, 92 events) unless the prospect gave you their list.

## 0:00 — Name the leak

> “You are not short on leads. You are short on follow-up. Every missed call is a homeowner
> who called the next roofer. Every unsold estimate is a decision that stalled on trust, not
> price. Every finished job is a review you did not ask for.”

Point at the three phases: **Stop the bleeding · Build trust · Close the job.**

## 1:00 — Show the money

On **Business**, point at:

- **Revenue recovered** — booked jobs, not “leads.”
- **Pipeline at stake** — the customer value sitting in the book.
- **Calls answered** — the calls the system handled.
- **Reviews to ask** — completed jobs with no review request.

> “This is the scoreboard. Not impressions. Not clicks. Booked jobs and revenue recovered.”

## 2:00 — Show the decision

Open **Today's queue**. Pick the top contact.

> “The OS is not choosing a message from a script. It is reading six resources — safety,
> trust, anticipation, clarity, agency, commitment — and finding the one that is actually
> blocking the sale.”

Point at the binding constraint, the move, the readiness, the channel.

> “Tom is blocked on trust, not price. So the OS confirms the quote and reassures him. It does
> not discount. Discounting a trust problem destroys margin and does not close the job.”

## 3:00 — Show the restraint

Point at the **pressure budget** and the rails.

> “Every person has a pressure budget. Every message spends some. When it is gone, the OS
> waits. It can choose ‘wait’ as a first-class decision.”

Point at **quiet hours**, **consent**, **claim trace**, **no review gating**, **human
handoff**.

> “This is why it does not feel like spam. The constraint is the product.”

## 4:00 — Run the day

Press **Run my day**. Read the result:

- actions taken,
- waits,
- blocked,
- value moved.

> “Eight decisions, one human handoff, two waits. The owner sees exactly what happened and
> why. Every decision is in the event log with a hash chain.”

## 5:00 — Show Hermes

Scroll to **Hermes · operations analyst**. Click **Weekly report** or ask:

> “List all six contacts in the top array — name, move, value, readiness.”

> “Hermes is the chief of staff. It reads the book and writes the plan. It cannot send, change
> consent, or book a job. The kernel decides; Hermes explains.”

## 6:00 — Show the kernel

Switch to **Kernel**. Show:

- the six-resource field,
- the alternatives list,
- the policy checks,
- the event log.

> “Every ‘yes’ and every ‘wait’ is traceable. If a customer asks ‘why did you text me?’, the
> answer is one event, not a model's mood.”

## 7:00 — Close

> “You have the book. We have the system that works it. Give us 30 days and your customer
> list. We will measure booked jobs against a holdout, and if we do not produce three booked
> jobs or $10,000, the next month is free.”

Then ask for the next step, not a maybe:

> “Send me the customer list and the Twilio number today. We will have the first ten messages
> on your phone for approval tomorrow.”

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “I already have a CRM.” | “Keep it. The CRM stores the book; Choice OS decides who to contact, why, and when. We can write back to it.” |
| “AI will spam my customers.” | “That is why there is a pressure budget, consent, quiet hours and a human handoff. The OS sends fewer messages than a blast, and the opt-out rate is the guardrail.” |
| “I tried an agency.” | “Agencies sell leads. This works the customers you already paid for. The pilot is measured against a holdout, not their dashboard.” |
| “Is this ethical?” | “No fake reviews, no invented deadlines, no purchased lists, no pressure on vulnerable people. The claim registry and policy rails are in the product, not the brochure.” |
| “Why $2,497?” | “One recovered roof or HVAC job usually covers the month. If we cannot recover three jobs in 30 days, the next month is free.” |
| “Will it work for my trade?” | “The kernel is vertical-neutral; the moves and claims are configured per trade. Roofing, HVAC, plumbing and restoration share the same decision structure.” |

## Demo hygiene

- Use `?static=1` only for screenshots; live demo should stream.
- Do not promise a calendar integration that is not connected. Say “booked in your calendar”
  only after the calendar adapter is live.
- Do not show real customer phone numbers or emails to a prospect.
- If Hermes is slow, say “it is thinking” and keep talking; do not click twice.
