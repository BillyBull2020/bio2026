# The 90-Day Operating Plan

Goal: **5 pilots, 3 retained, 2 published case studies, $7,500–$12,500 MRR.** One vertical
(roofing), one market (Denver metro), one offer (the 30-day revenue recovery pilot).

## Week 1 — Make it real

- [ ] Twilio number, 10DLC brand + campaign submitted.
- [ ] Resend domain verified; SPF, DKIM, DMARC pass.
- [ ] `setup-tenant` for the first pilot; `import-csv` the list.
- [ ] Claims registry: license, insurance, 2 case studies, real reviews.
- [ ] First 10 messages sent to the owner's phone for approval.
- [ ] STOP and quiet hours tested on a real phone.

**Exit:** one real message delivered to a real consented customer.

## Week 2 — First conversations

- [ ] 100 consented past customers in the reactivation queue.
- [ ] 25 unsold estimates / missed calls worked.
- [ ] Daily owner review of waits, blocks and opt-outs.
- [ ] Holdout set at 15%; experiment report running.
- [ ] First booked job.

**Exit:** at least 3 conversations and 1 booking. If not, inspect the offer and the list, not
the copy.

## Week 3 — Convert and prove

- [ ] Every completed job gets an honest review ask (no gate).
- [ ] Every price objection gets a human handoff within one business day.
- [ ] First before/after numbers: sends, replies, bookings, opt-outs, revenue.
- [ ] Fix the one claim or consent gap that blocked the most actions.
- [ ] Record a 60-second owner testimonial if the pilot is working.

**Exit:** at least 2 bookings and a clean opt-out rate (<2%).

## Week 4 — Decide

- [ ] Pull the holdout report.
- [ ] Write the one-page owner report: revenue recovered, pipeline, opt-outs, hours saved.
- [ ] Renewal conversation: continue at $2,497, or stop.
- [ ] Turn the pilot into a case study (with permission).
- [ ] Ask for two referrals.

**Exit:** pilot retained or a clean no. Either is a result; a maybe is not.

## Month 2 — Repeat, do not add features

- [ ] 3 more pilots from the same ICP.
- [ ] One onboarding checklist; one demo script; one report template.
- [ ] Calendar adapter for the bookings that need a hold.
- [ ] CRM write-back for the pilot that asks for it.
- [ ] Publish case study #1.

**Exit:** 4 active pilots, 2 case studies, $10k MRR.

## Month 3 — Tighten the machine

- [ ] Kill the lowest-converting move or phase.
- [ ] Raise the price to $3,497 for new customers after case study #2.
- [ ] Add a second market only if Denver is at capacity.
- [ ] Document the 30-minute onboarding and the day-30 report.
- [ ] Decide: bootstrap to 10 customers, or raise to hire a CSR.

**Exit:** 5–8 active pilots, 3 case studies, $12.5k–$20k MRR.

## Scorecard (review every Friday)

| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Consented contacts imported | 300+ per pilot | The book is the asset |
| Messages sent | 150–300 in month 1 | Enough to learn, not a blast |
| Reply rate | 8–15% | A specific reason beats volume |
| Booking rate | 3–6% of contacted | Revenue, not replies |
| Opt-out rate | **<2%** | The guardrail; above 2% stop and fix |
| Speed to lead | <5 minutes | The first responder wins |
| Revenue recovered | 3× fee | The only scoreboard |
| Owner hours saved | 5+ / week | The retention driver |

## Risks and the response

| Risk | Early signal | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Deliverability / 10DLC | Messages filtered, no replies | Pause, fix registration, warm the number |
| Consent gap | Most rows `needs_consent` | Do not send; collect consent or use email only |
| Opt-out spike | >2% in a week | Stop the campaign; inspect frequency and claims |
| No capacity | Bookings but no one answers | Pause the phase; the OS is not the bottleneck |
| Calendar gap | “Booked” but no slot | Add the calendar adapter before scaling |
| Owner ghosting | No approvals for 3 days | Escalate; a pilot without an owner is not a pilot |
| Price objection | “Too expensive” after demo | One recovered job pays the month; show the math |

## The one rule

Do not add a feature to avoid selling. The product is ready enough to prove the loop. The
constraint is **five real pilots**, not another integration.
