# First Week — Denver Roofing

The list is real: `outreach/denver-roofing-targets.csv` (20 companies, 13 with a public phone
number, OpenStreetMap sources). It is a **call list**, not a consent list. Business phones only;
respect the DNC and hang up on request.

## The only goal this week

Book **5 demos** and close **1 paid pilot**. Not “build more app.”

## Day 1 — call the 13 numbers

Use this, 45 seconds:

> “Hey, this is Billy with BioDynamX here in Denver. Quick question — when a call comes in
> while your crew is on a roof, who follows up?”
>
> *(Listen.)*
>
> “That’s the leak. We put a 30-day revenue recovery pilot on the calls and past customers you
> already have. No new leads, no list buying. If it doesn’t produce three booked jobs or
> $10,000 in 30 days, the next month is free. Can I send you the one-page audit?”

If yes: text/email `https://biodynamx.com/audit`. If no: “Fair. If a hail wave hits, call me
before you buy leads.”

## Day 1 — email the 20

Subject: `[Company] — missed calls`

> Hi [Name] —
>
> I’m Billy, local here in Denver. I work with roofing companies on one specific leak: the calls
> and past customers that never get followed up.
>
> Five questions will show the annual number at stake:
> https://biodynamx.com/audit
>
> If it’s useful, reply with a good time for 15 minutes. If not, ignore it.
>
> — Billy, BioDynamX

No attachments. No “AI transformation.” One link.

## Day 2 — follow up

- Call the 7 without a phone from their website contact form.
- Reply to any audit submission within **5 minutes**.
- Ask one question: “What happens today when a call is missed?”

## Day 3 — demo the number, not the software

1. Open their audit number.
2. Show the queue for a business like theirs.
3. Show one message and the approval.
4. Show the holdout.
5. Ask for the pilot.

Offer: **$1,500 setup + $2,497/mo**, 3 jobs/$10,000 guarantee. Stripe link when they say yes.

## Day 4–5 — close and onboard

- Send the pilot agreement and Stripe link the same day.
- Onboarding: connect the number, import the book, approve the first ten messages.
- First campaign live in **48 hours**.

## Tracking

Update the CSV columns:

| Column | Values |
|---|---|
| `status` | `not_contacted`, `called`, `emailed`, `replied`, `demo`, `pilot`, `lost` |
| `next_action` | the next concrete step |
| `notes` | what they said, in their words |

Do not add a lead to Choice OS as marketing-eligible until consent is captured. A call list is
not consent.
